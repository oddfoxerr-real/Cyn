# CYN Windows v2.0.73 — FxSound Attribution & License Clarity

- Rewrites the desktop **FxSound credits & CYN license** menu dialog to identify FxSound LLC and its contributors as original authors of the FxSound DSP, signed virtual driver, and upstream code.
- Separately identifies CYN interface, AI and app integrations as unofficial community modifications — not official FxSound releases or endorsements.
- Keeps the GNU Affero General Public License v3-or-later, source links, existing author notices, and the AGPL sections 15–17 no-warranty and liability disclaimer visible.
- Clarifies that attribution does not transfer responsibility for CYN modifications to FxSound and does not waive applicable distribution obligations.
- No changes to audio processing, models, device routing, presets, or themes.

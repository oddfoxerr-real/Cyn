# Cyn 2.0.21

- Autonomous Music AI combines scene detection and a trained Balance model to adjust incoming audio without requiring personal training examples.
- Bass, kick/snare balance and clarity adjustments support controlled boosts and cuts with silence and transient protection.
- New installations start with Smart listening and Music AI enabled. Existing saved preferences are preserved.
- Personal training remains optional. Audio analysis and inference run locally.

The Balance model was trained and evaluated on synthetic audio features; its validation scores do not establish real-song listening quality. This remains a preview release.

Install over your existing Cyn installation. The updater verifies the installer against the included SHA-256 checksum.

# CYN v2.0.69 — New Native Themes and UI Repairs

- Six genuinely new dark CYN themes: Aether Glass, Ion Violet, Liquid Titanium, Jade Circuit, Solar Ink and Polar Mist, each with new native role palettes and distinct procedural backdrop artwork, alongside CYN Black.
- Theme Studio previews the real selected material and saves compatible colors across all JUCE controls and the redesigned workspace.
- Corrected the compact Output Tuning screen: gain, leveling, filter Q, balance and band selection now use responsive workbench columns.
- Reworked AI DJ Touch coordinates to follow the actual native five-effect slider layout. Four flowers now claim distinct actively changing targets with a minimum 39-pixel separation and a full ranked candidate pool.
- The spectrum now shows a CYN-native multi-stop chromatic gradient.
- Smart Listening timeline retains up to six minutes of in-session audio, supports wheel scrolling through the captured history, and colors selected and newly taught intervals by Clarity, Ambience, Surround, Dynamics or Bass.
- Keeps existing DSP, EQ filter counts, models, output routing, reduced-motion support and previous close animation options.

Timeline history and color annotations are in-memory for the current app session, not archival training history.
